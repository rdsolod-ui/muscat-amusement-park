# MAP v5 — content evidence and image provenance

Research checked 2 October 2026. This note records the basis for presentation copy; it does not establish an investment agreement, funding award or planning approval.

## ASAAS and Oman Vision 2040

**ASAAS / أساس** is the Muscat National Development and Investment Company. Its corporate description covers development, investments, co-investments and public/private collaboration. It is a relevant prospective investment partner; no ASAAS commitment to MAP has been established. [ASAAS Company](https://asaas.om/en/company/)

**Oman Vision 2040 is the national framework.** The Ministry of Heritage and Tourism describes tourism as a priority for diversification, quality visitor experiences, sustainability and community benefit. MAP's proposed contribution should be described as an ambition aligned with that framework, not as an officially endorsed programme. [Ministry of Heritage and Tourism, 16 April 2025](https://mht.gov.om/media-center/news/oman-strengthens-tourism-sector-with-expansion-plans-and-global-connectivity-initiatives/)

ASAAS's tourism webpage references a 2040 horizon using historical sector data attributed to Oxford Business Group in 2015. This is not evidence of a separate current “ASAAS 2040 plan” or a MAP subsidy programme. Its dated numerical targets are not used in the new closing slide. [ASAAS Tourism](https://asaas.om/en/sectors/tourism/)

ASAAS also publicly presents A’Sharq with hospitality, water park, theme park and retail/dining components. The inspected page does not establish its completion. This supports the relevance of the leisure sector to ASAAS; it also cautions against unsupported national “first” claims for MAP. [A’Sharq](https://asaas.om/en/asharq-eng/)

## Requested municipal support

The proposed close asks for three concrete actions:

1. Support site allocation and long-term land-use rights through the applicable process.
2. Facilitate an investment partnership review with ASAAS and relevant financing partners.
3. Review eligibility for available incentives and support with the competent authorities.

Invest Oman describes tourism investment, public/private partnerships and land-use/incentive mechanisms. Their applicability to MAP, its proposed site and its legal/development structure remains to be assessed. General investment information is not a project-specific offer, guaranteed subsidy or approved land right. [Invest Oman — Tourism](https://www.investoman.om/start-investing/sectors/tourism-38)

## MG Group credentials

MG group's corporate portfolio includes the Ferris wheel in Salalah. This supports wording about the company's experience and the wheel appearing in its Oman portfolio. The precise contractual delivery role is not established by that webpage alone. [MG group — Oman Ferris wheel](https://vsevpark.com/omanferriswheel)

The promoter's current statement concerning eight parks is supplied project information. It is not independently verified by this research and is not a world-ranking claim. The public corporate homepage was not used as proof of the current eight-park figure. [MG group](https://vsevpark.com/)

Claims that the Salalah wheel is Oman's first, or that MAP is Oman's first or largest park, are not established by the inspected sources. The proposed wording is **“a new landmark family destination for Muscat.”** Future ambition and verified delivery credentials remain distinct.

## User-supplied Salalah photographs

The project team supplied a set of original DJI drone files showing the actual wheel and coastline. Fourteen DNG files were inspected through exact extraction of their camera-embedded JPEG previews. Original file hashes were checked before and after extraction; sources were unchanged.

The selected framing is source **0035**: the complete wheel and its foundations, coast and surrounding urban context are visible without the foreground tower obstructing the composition. Source **0005** provides a closer alternative, with a more tightly cropped base. Embedded previews are 960×540; the DNG's native image is larger and the preview should not be mistaken for full-resolution RAW development.

Any generated day, sunset or night variations based on this source are **lighting interpretations of a supplied photograph**, not separate documentary shoots or proof of the original time of day. Preserve the actual geometry, coast and surroundings; distinguish the original photograph from the interpreted variations in the asset record.
