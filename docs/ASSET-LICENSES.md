# Asset licence boundaries

- Project-original concept designs, supplied attraction geometry, renderings and the editable Blender scene are publicly available for review. No blanket MIT, CC0 or other open asset licence is declared for them.
- NASA imagery, Natural Earth geography, Poly Haven source resources and font software retain their individual source notices. These notices apply only to those resources, not the entire park design or website.
- The public source masterplan is a vector redraw without the original map screenshot. The public Blender scene excludes the Esri context image. Separate geographic reference imagery used by the website retains its provider rights; preserve visible credits and the relevant terms when reusing or republishing those images.
- Generated illustrations are concept visuals. They are not documentary evidence of construction or an approved engineering plan.
- Software dependencies retain their respective licences recorded in the lockfile and installed package notices.

No broad software or asset licence is added on behalf of the project owner. Public source publication is separate from website deployment and from official project approval.
