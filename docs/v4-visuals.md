The Google satellite image was neither generated nor passed into image generation. It is preserved separately with original attribution; see `v4-site/README.md`.

Pins in the first view are image-registered; Blender pins are camera-projected. The generated view is an artistic interpretation and has its own visually inspected pin positions in `src/data/masterplan-frames-v4.json`. It is not a geometrically exact or surveyed engineering drawing. No images are reused as main artwork in different chapters.
